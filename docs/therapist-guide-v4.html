What’s New in the ABA Tracker

A quick guide for therapists — how to pause, resume, and never lose your data.

Version 4

⏸️ Pause a session anytime

Need to stop in the middle — traveling to another location, a patient break, the bathroom, or you lost internet? Just pause. Your data and your time are saved.

  • 1 Tap ⏸ Pause at the top of the session.
  • 2 Pick a reason if you want (optional).
  • 3 Tap Pause & Save. You’re done for now.

▶️ Resume where you left off

Your paused session waits for you — on any device. Start on your phone, finish on your tablet.

  • 1 Go to the client screen after logging in.
  • 2 Tap your Paused session card at the top.
  • 3 Everything is exactly as you left it. Keep going!

📶 Works without internet

No signal? Keep collecting data normally — the app saves everything on your device.

When you tap Submit with no connection, the session is safely stored and sends itself automatically the moment you’re back online.

💾 Your data is protected

The app auto-saves every few seconds while you work.

If your phone closes, dies, or refreshes, you’ll see a “Recovered” card next time you log in so you can finish the session — nothing is lost.

⏱️ Your session time stays accurate

Time spent paused (travel, breaks) is not counted in the session length. The recorded therapy time reflects the actual time you spent with the client — so your hours are always correct.

💡 Good to know

  • You can pause and resume as many times as you need in one session.
  • If you’re offline with a paused or not-yet-sent session, don’t log out — the app will warn you, because logging out could lose it. Wait until you’re back online.
  • A green “synced” message means your paused session is safely on the server and available on other devices.
